My personal website serves as a portfolio and online presence, showcasing my various projects and skills. It includes examples of web development and software projects, engineering and DIY projects, as well as a photography gallery. Built using technologies such as Strapi, NextJS, TailwindCSS, Docker, and hosted on a Linux server, this website is a reflection of my diverse interests and capabilities. Whether it's browsing through my blog posts or exploring my project showcases, my personal site is a central hub for displaying my work and sharing my passions.
